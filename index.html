FAQ

Common questions

Scuto is the first AI web scraper with built-in GDPR compliance. While other tools extract everything, including personal data, Scuto automatically detects and scrubs PII (emails, phone numbers, addresses, and 8 categories of personal data) before you export. Your data stays clean and compliant by default.

Scuto is a universal web scraper, so it needs to read page content on any website you visit. This permission is only used when you actively trigger an extraction. Scuto never collects data in the background, never tracks your browsing, and never sends raw HTML to external servers. The AI processes a cleaned, anonymized version of the page.

Scuto works on any website. It comes with 20+ pre-configured sites (Amazon, eBay, LinkedIn Jobs, Airbnb, Indeed, Zillow, and more) for one-click extraction. For any other site, just describe what data you need in plain English, and the AI will figure out the structure.

No raw page data is stored on our servers. The page content is cleaned and converted to a minimal format (removing scripts, ads, and personal data) before being sent to the AI for structure analysis. The AI response (your extracted data) is returned directly to your browser. We only store usage metadata (extraction count, timestamps) for quota management.
